About

Background & Focus

I'm a Junior Data Analyst with an academic background in Statistics and Medical Records, currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Health Informatics at Beni-Suef University. My early healthcare experience began with a six-month internship at the Minya Health Directorate, where I supported data entry, medical records, statistics, and administrative reporting. In the Planning & Follow-up Department, I used Microsoft Excel and Word to organize information and prepare documents, and I also worked with the payroll system in the Accounts Department.

I work with SQL, Power BI, DAX, Advanced Excel, Power Query, and Power Pivot to clean, structure, analyze, and visualize data. My focus is on transforming raw information into reliable insights that help teams monitor performance, identify patterns, and make better-informed decisions.

Healthcare is where my domain knowledge began, but my analytical approach extends beyond healthcare. I'm interested in applying data analytics to business and operational challenges across different industries, while continuously developing my skills in Python, AI-assisted analytics, and workflow automation.

Data only becomes valuable when it drives action — turning raw numbers into decisions that matter.
